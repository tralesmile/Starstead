# Starstead: Design (owner's choices, NEVER cut, only reorder)

Working title: **Starstead** (star + homestead). Single-player first (multiplayer maybe after launch).

## Pitch
Open-world life sim in detailed top-down (3/4 view) pixel art. Start on a run-down homestead; live any life: farmer, fisher, racer, builder, worker, athlete, or criminal. Levelling up carries the same life to the Moon, then to other planets, each a new open world with new animals, jobs, laws and economy.
Hook: Stardew Valley's heart, GTA's freedom, a rocket at the end of the road.

## Owner's feature list (all must be kept)
- Big open-world map, real-world-like but going beyond it; a story.
- Farming, fishing, cutting trees, mining (incl. caves).
- Animals around the world: catch them, keep your own, get resources (milk, wool, eggs, hides), tame and ride them, put clothes on animals.
- Other planets with different animals (dinosaurs etc.); Moon and planets are open worlds unlocked as higher levels.
- Jobs and careers (many real jobs, promotions, own business); sports (racing, football, basketball, boxing, fishing contests, animal races, later low-gravity sports).
- Sell what you grow or build; buy weapons, food, cars, clothes, everything.
- Build anything: house, farm, barns, shops, garages, workshops, sports venues; build cars, weapons, equipment; later Moon bases and your own rocket.
- Combat; driving cars; racing.
- Enter existing houses and rob them (NPCs remember, change locks, get dogs); police.
- Begin single-player.

## Differentiators (our hooks)
1. Interplanetary trade (crops/minerals priced differently per world; smuggling).
2. Clothes on animals are gear (fur coat = ice planet, armour, racing saddle).
3. The world remembers (NPC homes, routines, grudges, revenge jobs).
4. Two reputations: honest vs street, both viable, can mix.
5. Build it, then use it (cars, weapons, finally a rocket from mined/grown/stolen parts).
6. Each planet = new map, animals, jobs, laws, sports.
7. Your home travels with you (house parts become a ship module).

## Story
Inherit grandmother's broken homestead near Port Halden, and her debt to the Vance family (legit companies by day, crime by night). Vance Aerospace is secretly building a rocket; grandmother's notes say she found something on the Moon first.
Acts: 1 Earth Homestead; 2 Earth City (honest/street rep, caves, circuit, heists, houses, rocket seat); 3 Moon; 4 Other planets (jungle/dinosaurs, ice, desert, own rocket); 5 Endgame (trade routes, businesses on every planet, story ending).

## Art direction (reference: owner screenshot in docs/reference/ if present)
2D high-detail top-down 3/4 pixel art. Painterly shading, layered grass and moss, deep evergreen trees, see-through water darkening with depth (lily pads, reeds, visible fish), warm wooden props (docks, rope rails), chunky friendly characters, yellow outline on interactables. 32x32 tiles, 640x360 base resolution scaled by integer factors, day/night lighting, weather. Palette per world: Earth green/gold, Moon grey/blue, jungle purple.

## Tech
Godot 4 (GDScript), Aseprite-style pixel art (generate placeholder art in code/PNG until final art), Git, GUT tests, Steam page at vertical slice. Only CC0/free-license assets; record in CREDITS.txt.
Age rating note: crime and violence likely 16+/18+; keep tone cartoony-gritty and decide early.
